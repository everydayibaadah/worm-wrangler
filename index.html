Worm Wrangler

How to Play

Objective: Catch as many worms as you can before time runs out or you miss too many!

  • Click on the worms as they pop out of the holes.
  • Different difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard) change the game speed, timer, and miss limits.
  • Golden Worms: Rare and worth extra points!
  • Time Worms: Catch these to add a few precious seconds to your timer.
  • Bombs: Avoid clicking these! They'll cost you points and misses.

Select your glove, choose your difficulty, and start wrangling!
